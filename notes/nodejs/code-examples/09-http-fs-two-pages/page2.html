About Apples

Apples (Malus domestica) are one of the most popular fruits worldwide. They are rich in fiber, vitamin C, and antioxidants.

Calories52 kcal

Fun Facts

  • There are over 7,500 varieties of apples grown worldwide.
  • India’s famous apple regions include Himachal Pradesh and Kashmir.
  • Apples are used in pies, juices, cider, and vinegar.
